## v0.79.37
- Card packs is lit by the same lantern as the main menu. It had a room of its own, and walking from one to the other read as changing building; now it is one room, and what changes is what you find in it.
- Half the pack row did not respond to the mouse. The strip that holds the Collect button runs the full width of the screen and sits over the row: the button inside it was hidden, but the strip itself was still catching every click that landed on it.
- "Next in 3h 42m" is written smaller and dimmer than a pack name. It is a wait, not the name of something you own, and at the same size it read as one.

## v0.79.36
- "Card packs" is a different page. Packs are things you own now: a timed one on the left, everything else on the right, in a row that scrolls once you have more than four. You pick the one you want to open instead of opening "the pack".
- There are three kinds. A daily pack matures on its own every 12 hours (it was 8) and does not stack — the clock restarts when you open it, not when it ripens, so leaving it there costs you nothing. A reward pack is the one you win by playing. A treasure pack is the one you buy, 100 magic ink, with the yellow button on the right.
- The Dark/Light switch moved up into the title bar, and says which version you are looking at instead of which one you would get. It used to appear for a moment between the two cards and say the opposite thing depending on when you read it.
- Opening, the cards, and Collect are exactly what they were. The page around them changed; the sequence did not.

## v0.79.35
- Card art now loads whatever format you saved it in: .jpg, .png or .jpeg. The game only ever looked for .jpg, and it did so silently — it asked the site for an address that does not exist, found nothing, and left the placeholder. Five characters had their art sitting on the site in .png and had never once been seen.
- The three formats cost nothing at startup. Which illustrations exist is worked out once, at import, and written into the catalogue; what you download is the answer. Only cards still without art get checked again while you play, and those are exactly the ones the two extra formats are for.

## v0.79.34
- "Forgot password?" works. Type your email, we send you a 6 digit code — the same email as the activation one — and with it you choose a new password. It expires in an hour.
- The dust in the login screen is blurred like the dust in the main menu. The two extreme layers were swapped: the background grains, which are tiny, were being blurred until they went out, and the foreground ones, which are large, were left almost sharp. It read as "the login dust is not blurred", and it was right.

## v0.79.33
- Every window in the game now fades in when it opens and fades out when it closes, over 150 milliseconds. They used to appear and vanish in a single frame, which on a full screen reads as a jolt rather than as something opening.
- On the starter deck screen the three letters arrive one after the other, just after the window itself. Three things appearing together read as one block; three arriving in turn are read one at a time, which is what you want of three letters you have to choose between.
- And the title bar of that screen is 600 pixels wide with the title in the middle, instead of running the full width of the three letters.

## v0.79.32
- Searching for an opponent now requires an activated account. The game already kept you at the activation screen, but that was the game being polite; this is the server saying no, on the door to the queue itself.
- And if the server ever refuses a search, the game says so instead of leaving the spinner turning. That branch simply did not exist: nothing was wrong, nothing happened, and you waited.

## v0.79.31
- Creating an account now ends with a 6 digit code. Press "Confirm and create" and the window turns into the activation screen: type the code we email you, press "Complete registration", and you are back at the login with an account that is really yours. It confirms the mailbox is real and belongs to you.
- Close the window before typing it and nothing is lost: log in again and the same screen is waiting, with a button to send a new code. The code lasts 24 hours.
- The email is the one Lorenzo designed, with the same six boxes you see in the game.
- The title bar of every window is drawn with the new art, tiled across the whole width.

## v0.79.30
- Little Mermaid gains her +2 to every side again. Her card says "Gains +2 ALL when played, but discards a card from your hand", and only the discard was happening. The two halves of that sentence are handled by two different parts of the game, and when one of them was not something the engine could work out on its own, both were dropped — so the half it could do perfectly well went with it.

## v0.79.29
- The AI now brings a deck that costs what yours costs. Deck capacity is the sum of the rarity costs — timeless 4, mythic 3, rare 2, common 1 — and the game gives you 24 points to spend. The AI was spending all 24 every single game, whoever it was playing: against a starter deck it came to the table with twelve rares. It now spends exactly what you spent.
- The old balancing did exist, but it matched the wrong thing: card power, which is the sum of the numbers on the sides. Power cannot see abilities, and abilities are most of what separates a common from a rare — so two decks could balance on paper and not be the same game at all.

## v0.79.28
- The damage bubbles are gone. The drawings they were dressed in had been deleted, so every match was asking the site for four files that no longer exist; the bubbles themselves had stopped flying back when scoring replaced damage, and only the machinery was left. Nothing about the score changed: the difference between attacker and defender, and one point for each of your own cards on the board at the end of a turn, are counted exactly as before.
- The specular sheen is back on cards above level 1. In the full-screen card view the arrows redraw the card at the level you pick, but the sheen was still asking the card its real level — so stepping up to 2, 3 or 4 showed the foil and the parallax and never the sheen.
- The red dot on "Library & decks" now sits at the right end of the button instead of next to the words, which it used to push off-centre.
- And it goes away once you have seen the new cards. It cleared only for cards the mouse happened to pass over, one at a time, and a collection is read by scrolling — so it stayed on forever. Now a card counts as seen when it has actually been on your screen.
- The two lines inside "Card packs" sit 2 pixels apart. They were written to be 0 and were drawn at 10: a stronger rule further down the stylesheet was quietly overriding it.
