## v0.81.17
- New artwork for 30 cards.
- 12 Dancing Princesses now always gets her move after a conquest. If the turn timer ran out while the clash was still playing out, she used to stay put without a word.
- Dragging a card into a deck now fades the copy left behind in the library.

## v0.81.16
- With "Show all" ticked, cards you don't own could be dropped into a deck. They can't any more.

## v0.81.15
- New artwork for 47 cards.
- A few cards were still showing their old illustration, or none at all. Fixed.

## v0.81.14
- Minor bug fixes and improvements

## v0.81.13
- Improved the Library & decks searchbar and filters: you can now search by ability name, ability text and trait, there is a new "Ability" filter, and "Show all" is a checkbox.

## v0.81.12
- Spending Magic Ink or Fairy Dust now slides in a small panel showing your balance going down.
- Hovering a face-down card while opening a pack no longer gives away its traits.
- The glow behind a card levelling up is no longer clipped, and the flip sound now lands with the flip.

## v0.81.11
- The pack's charge-up sound now closes out the moment the pack tears open, instead of playing on underneath.

## v0.81.10
- The sound of a pack tearing open is back, and lands exactly when the pack opens.

## v0.81.9
- Fixed a rare but serious fault that could reset a player's collection, currency and level to those of a brand new account.
- The sound a pack makes while it charges up is now heard all the way to the end.
- The sparks from a flipped card are a little smaller, and now come in two kinds: sharp ones and glowing ones.

## v0.81.8
- Fixed a few cards showing no illustration at all.
- The sparks thrown out by a card you flip in a pack are four times bigger, and there are twice as many of them.
- The pack's charge-up sound is no longer drowned out by the tear.

## v0.81.7
- Flipping a card in a pack now throws out sparks in its rarity's colour: a handful for a Common, twice as many for each step up, and a cloud of forty for a Timeless.
- The pack now takes a moment longer to tear open, so its charge-up sound has room to be heard.
- New art for a few more cards.

## v0.81.6
- The sound that plays while a pack is charging up is now heard in full: the pack shakes for exactly as long as the sound lasts.
- New art for a few more cards.

## v0.81.5
- The full card view during a match now shows how many board points that card scores, in place of its Power cost.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements
