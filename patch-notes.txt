## v0.79.49
- The login screen is in order: email, password, Forgot password, the "or" line, Google, Remember me, Login. Below the panel, where they belong, sit the two things that are not signing in — Create new account and Exit game. Google moved back inside the panel: it used to sit outside so it would show on the sign-up screen too, but nobody creating an account with Google fills in those fields first.
- Tick Remember me and the next launch shows you nothing at all: no logo, no login panel, straight to the main menu. The check used to run at the END of the opening sequence, so the whole screen faded in and was taken away again a moment later. It now leaves with the loading bar and the answer is usually back before there is anything to show.
- A ticked box is ten pixels bigger than an unticked one, everywhere in the game. It grows from the centre, so nothing around it moves.
- Text fields are 16px and the Remember me line is 14px.

## v0.79.48
- Aeolus is a usable name again. The profanity list comes from outside the game and has some noise in it — the god of the winds was in there, and so is the word for a bookmaker. There is a place in the filter for exactly this, and now it has its first entry.

## v0.79.47
- There is a Remember me box on the login screen, and ticking it keeps you signed in for a month. It is off to begin with, and while it is off the game still writes nothing at all into your browser. Ticked, it writes one thing: the token that renews your session, and the address it belongs to — not your balance, not your decks, not your cards, which stay on the server as they always have.
- It is safe to use with two windows open, which is why it took this long. The old version of this saved you in a box shared by the whole browser and READ from it whenever it liked: sign in as somebody else in a second window and the first window quietly became them too. Now that box is read once, at startup, before there is any identity to confuse — after that each window has its own session and never looks at the box again.
- Signing out, or deleting your account, forgets you as well.

## v0.79.46
- Player names go through a profanity filter now, and it lives on the server — the only place a rule is a rule. It is deliberately soft: the full list is only matched against whole words, so Assatanato, Cassandra, Banal and peacock all pass, while a couple of dozen unmistakable words are caught anywhere in the name, so fuckyou and adolfhitler do not. Names the game itself prints on cards can never be banned: Puss, Babes and Nymph are card names, and they are read from the catalogue rather than typed into a list somebody has to remember to update.
- Two new ways to sort the collection: Highest single power puts the biggest number on any one side first, Highest overall power puts the biggest total first. They are never the same order — a 1-1-23 card wins single and loses overall.
- The last tap on a wax seal has its own sound. The first four are hammer blows; the fifth is the wax giving way, and that is a different thing.
- The little arrow in the Sort by box is back. There were four dropdowns in the game and the arrow was mounted by whoever built the window around them: two did it, the filters panel did not, so it was an empty img tag — invisible, with nothing anywhere to say so. Now one place dresses them all.

## v0.79.45
- Looking at a new card now actually clears it. Two functions were not looking up the same key: cards can be named two ways in the game, one function accepted both and the other always answered with the first, so when the list knew a card by the other name the card lit up and nothing could put it out. The dot stayed on forever.
- The magic ink panel slides in showing what you had, not what you are left with. It entered already reduced and then counted down to the same number, which showed the result twice and the payment never.
- And it counts down from the right currency. It was reading the fairy dust balance, left over from when the second card was paid in dust: with 9999 dust and 250 ink it counted from 9999 to 200.

## v0.79.44
- When you pay for a second card, the magic ink panel slides back in from off the edge of the screen, then flashes and counts the sum down. It used to flash and count while it was still hidden with the rest of the page, so by the time the page came back the number had simply changed and you never saw yourself pay.
- The plus on that panel is the same one as in the main menu.

## v0.79.43
- The version number is on the Big Fennel splash too, in the same corner. It is written in one place and copied to the other, so there is still only one number to change.

## v0.79.42
- Keeping a second card costs the price of the CHEAPER of the two, whichever order you pick them in. For two versions it was the price of the one you picked second, which turned the order of your clicks into a lever: the same pair cost 50 or 200 depending on which you touched first. The button now shows what you will actually pay, and the server charges the same.
- The glow and the lit border behind an unpacked card lean with the card. They have their own layer that was reading the tilt from a different branch of the page, so they always saw zero and stood still.
- Turning a card throws sparks out from behind it, along a winding path rather than a straight line, fast at first and slowing as they fade. How many, what colour and how long they last is the card rarity: a handful of white for a common, a cloud of orange for a timeless.
- Dropping an envelope in the middle now travels there, fast then slowing, instead of drifting in on a spring. A spring is the right motion for chasing a moving pointer; aimed at a fixed target it reads as hesitation.
- The label above a card is frosted glass like every other panel in the game.
- The burst of light is another fifth larger.
- The new-cards dot could get stuck on forever. A card counts as new until you look at it, and you can only look at it if it is in your collection — so a card left in your account whose name the sheet no longer knows was new for good, with no gesture able to clear it. Cards that cannot be looked at no longer count. There is also a debug button that marks everything as seen.

## v0.79.41
- The cards leave the screen faster after Collect: the rise is 30% quicker, while the little dip that sets it up is unchanged. The dip is the wind-up; shortening it would have taken the spring out instead of adding it.
- The envelope is gone before the cards come back. It used to fade out from 750 to 1100 milliseconds, and the cards start returning at 550 — so it was dissolving underneath them. It now starts at 150 and is gone at 500.
- The burst of light is a fifth larger.

## v0.79.40
- The burst of light when a pack opens is in front of the envelope. It was behind both halves of it, so the light was born inside the paper and stayed there — you only saw what spilled past the edges.
- The sparks are warm now (the colour of the lantern rather than plain white) and half again as blurred, so they read as points of light instead of dots.
- Press Collect and the cards drop a little before they shoot off the top, in half the time they used to take. The sound waits for the moment they turn and go up.
- The card labels fade off every card the moment you press Collect. They answered their question; the choice is made.
- The second magic ink box is gone. There is one balance on this page, at the left edge, and it no longer leaves a leftover panel behind when the rest of the page steps aside.
- The white glow on a pack in the row is gone. The row clips its own contents, so the glow on the first and last pack was cut off square against the panel edge — worse than no glow.
