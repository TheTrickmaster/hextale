## v0.81.9
- Fixed a rare but serious fault that could reset a player's collection, currency and level to those of a brand new account.
- The sound a pack makes while it charges up is now heard all the way to the end.
- The sparks from a flipped card are a little smaller, and now come in two kinds: sharp ones and glowing ones.

## v0.81.8
- Fixed a few cards showing no illustration at all.
- The sparks thrown out by a card you flip in a pack are four times bigger, and there are twice as many of them.
- The pack's charge-up sound is no longer drowned out by the tear.

## v0.81.7
- Flipping a card in a pack now throws out sparks in its rarity's colour: a handful for a Common, twice as many for each step up, and a cloud of forty for a Timeless.
- The pack now takes a moment longer to tear open, so its charge-up sound has room to be heard.
- New art for a few more cards.

## v0.81.6
- The sound that plays while a pack is charging up is now heard in full: the pack shakes for exactly as long as the sound lasts.
- New art for a few more cards.

## v0.81.5
- The full card view during a match now shows how many board points that card scores, in place of its Power cost.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements

## v0.81.4
- New sounds for the end of a match.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements

## v0.81.3
- The tooltip of a card on the board now shows its rarity and how many board points it scores each turn.
- A bubble with the points you just made now pops up on the board: between the two cards when you conquer one, and on every card that scores at the end of a turn.
- New art for a first batch of cards.

## v0.81.2
- Opening a pack is much quicker now: cards arrive sooner and the collect animation is faster.
- You can review every tutorial from Settings.
- Volume settings are now remembered by your device and apply the moment the game opens.
- A new modal announces the start of a new season, and another one tells you about your welcome gift.
- Board tiles now flip into place instead of falling.

## v0.81.1
- Minor bug fixes and improvements

## v0.81.0
- A welcome gift: 10 card packs for new players, 4 for everyone already here, and 1000 Magic Ink for all.
- Winning against a bot now gives you 3 Magic Ink.
- No more turn timer when you play against a bot from the menu. Matches found through matchmaking keep it.
- Abilities that add or remove RAND Power now roll a separate side for every single point, instead of stacking it all on one.
- Cards now show how much Power capacity they cost while you build a deck, and the card view has a new "Power cost" line.
- The season is shown above your rank in the main menu.
- Faster startup, music from the login screen, and a snappier card drag.
