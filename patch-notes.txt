## v0.79.56
- Cards on the board are worth points at the end of every turn according to their rarity, and the scale runs backwards: a common is worth 3, a rare 2, a mythic 1, a timeless nothing. Two commons, a mythic and a timeless on the board is seven points.
- So a hand of common cards is a choice now, not a consolation. Fill the board with legendaries and you win the exchanges and score nothing; fill it with commons and you score and lose the exchanges. The board is the question.
- A timeless card does not flash in the end-of-turn wave at all. Lighting it up while the counter stayed put would read as a fault rather than as the rule.

## v0.79.52
- The catalogue has been rebuilt and now knows all 109 illustrations and all 43 battlecries, so the game reads them instead of hunting for them: the art is there the moment the Library opens, for every card, and the cards shout again.
- One more guard of my own had to go. Yesterday I made the game distrust a catalogue that carried empty voice lists, because an empty list was the fingerprint of the failed import. But no card in this game has numbered voice lines — only battlecries — so an empty list is the true answer, and a perfect catalogue would have been distrusted too. The question is now the right one: did the import look for audio at all?

## v0.79.51
- Found why four cards showed their art instantly and the other hundred took several seconds. The game looks for a file next to itself first and on the website second, and the "next to itself" half had been pointing at a folder that does not exist ever since the game moved into its own directory. Everything went to the network, including the tool that builds the card catalogue: it asked the website about 1300 questions in one breath, the website started answering "too many requests", and the tool could not tell that answer apart from "there is no such file". So it wrote down that 107 cards have no illustration and that no card has a battlecry, and every player has been rediscovering the truth the slow way at every launch, one throttled request at a time.
- The four instant ones were simply the four that squeezed through before the throttling began.
- The game now warns in its console if the local folder never answers, instead of quietly downloading everything it already has on disk.

## v0.79.50
- Cards have their voices back. Every battlecry and every voice line had gone silent, and not because of the sound: the loading pass that discovers art and audio takes a shortcut when the imported catalogue already knows the answers, and it decided the catalogue knew by asking whether the field was there rather than whether anything was in it. The field was there for all 111 cards, empty for all 111. Art kept working, so nothing looked wrong.
- The Google button is ours now. It used to be drawn by Google inside a frame we could not style — the one button on the screen that came from somewhere else. Signing in now takes the route Google itself points to for a custom button, which hands us a code instead of a token; the code is worth nothing on its own and is exchanged on our server, so the secret that exchanges it never travels.
- The login panel is in its final order: email, password, then Remember me and Forgot password sharing a line, then Login. The "or" divider and Google sit at the bottom, after the way in you probably came for.
