## v0.80.4
- Badr al-Budur's ability now works: when played, choose an adjacent enemy to swap places with, and she attacks from her new position.

## v0.80.3
- Magic Mirror no longer turns into The Green Prince when it copies from a Frog Prince that has already transformed.
- A card pulled by Rapunzel no longer jumps backwards for a moment when the pull starts.

## v0.80.2
- The "Lv up" banner in Library & Decks now tilts together with its card.
- Freezing a card or a tile now shows the ice forming, and the ice on a frozen card moves and tilts with the card.
- Card tooltips no longer list buffs and debuffs without saying where they come from.

## v0.80.1
- In online matches you can no longer be matched against yourself when your account is searching from two windows or devices at once.
- The "Right-click to expand hand" hint now always stays on your side of the screen in online matches.
- Daily quests now update in the main menu after a match. Before, only the popups showed your progress.
- Carabosse, and any ability that weakens a random side, no longer targets shielded sides, like Pinocchio's shield or The Crystal Princess's crown.
- Points from two hits landing close together are no longer lost, so the right victory or defeat music now plays at the end of a match.

## v0.80.0
- The turn timer bar now empties smoothly during your turn, stops while the turn changes and refills instantly. It no longer jumps back and forth.
- Abilities that pick something at random, like The Genie, now roll again every time they trigger. A card that copies or steals one no longer buffs the same values.
- Random effects are no longer shown before you play a card: the values they could change show a "?" instead.
- The "?" on light cards now has a white outline.
- In online matches, Mad Hatter's random value is now the same on both screens.

## v0.79.90
- Cards now have levels. Every copy of a card you keep from a pack counts toward its next level: 2 copies for level 2, 5 for level 3 and 9 for level 4.
- Level up your cards from Library & Decks: cards ready to level up show a "Lv up" banner. Open the card and spend Magic Ink to raise its Power, and at some levels unlock its ability.
- Leveling up costs 50, 150 and 400 Magic Ink for Common cards, 100, 300 and 800 for Rare, 150, 450 and 1200 for Mythic, and 200, 600 and 1600 for Timeless.
- Starter deck cards count as one copy each. If you already had duplicates, your cards have been raised to the level they earned, for free.
- Once a card has all 9 copies, any further copy you keep turns into Magic Ink.
- When you open a pack, each card shows its level and how many copies it needs for the next one.
- Card levels apply in online matches too: each of your opponent's cards plays at its own level.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements
