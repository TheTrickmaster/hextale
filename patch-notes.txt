- Fixed, and it was a serious one: two windows open with two different accounts shared the same sign-in, so decks, levels, cards and currency from one account showed up in the other.
- Signing in is now per window and lasts as long as the window. You will be asked for your password each time you open the game — that is the price of never playing, or spending, on somebody else's account.
- The free pack's eight hours are now counted by the server, so clearing your browser data no longer earns you an extra pack, and two accounts on one computer no longer take the wait from each other.

## v0.77.89
- The game now loads what it will actually use: your real card list arrives while the loading bar is still running, so the artwork is ready when you reach the main menu instead of filling in afterwards.
- Signing in is immediate. Your avatar, level, experience, decks and card art are already there.
- The deck you had selected is the one you find selected next time, "random deck" included.
- Buff and malus are explained again when you hover a card or open it: one line per source, green when it raises a value and red when it lowers it. Abilities described in the card sheet are now included, which is most of them.
- Fixed: when you played as the second player, your deck pile and the "expand hand" hint stayed on your opponent's side of the screen.
- Attack: the landing bounce can no longer restart on top of the strike.

## v0.77.88
- Fixed: Magic Mirror copied an ability but never used it. A copied or stolen ability now runs exactly as if the card had just been played, and the card it was taken from really loses it.
- Fixed: March Hare's swap played its animation and then quietly put the two values back where they were.
- Fixed: some cards showed no preview of their values while you dragged them over the board. Princess and the Pea, whose bonus depends on where you put her, showed nothing at all.
- Fixed: the expanded hand view was pushed off the left edge of the screen for one of the two players. Both players now see their expanded cards in the middle.
- Fixed: your deck's colours could show up on your opponent's side of an online match.
- When an ability finds nobody to target, the game now says which neighbours it looked at and why it passed on each of them.

## v0.77.87
- Online: your cards and your opponent's now go onto the board at the level each of you really has, so the numbers on their sides are the true ones.
- The "match ended" notice now only appears when someone actually leaves. A match that ends normally goes straight to the result.

## v0.77.86
- Fixed: in an online match, an ability that asks you to pick a target refused your pick and left the window open until the time ran out.
- Fixed: your name and avatar could appear on the wrong side of the board.

## v0.77.85
- You always play on the left. Your opponent — a person or the computer — is always on the right, whichever player number the server gave you.
- Your opponent's hand stays face down when a match ends or is interrupted.
- If a match has to be stopped, the notice now says what happened and takes you back to the main menu on a ten-second countdown.

## v0.77.84
- The three starter decks now have names: Starter Wild, Starter Debuff and Starter Princess, and each arrives ready to play.
- Your cards now go into a match at the level YOU have them at, not at whatever level the card list happened to carry.
- New accounts start their cards at level 1.

## v0.77.83
- Your avatar is always one of the in-game avatars. The game never takes the picture from the account you signed in with.
- The battle music now falls silent for a victory or defeat jingle instead of just turning down.
- Your name in the main menu refreshes as soon as you change it.

## v0.77.82
- The dust particles on the start screen no longer stretch out of shape on unusual window sizes.

## v0.77.81
- Online: abilities that ask you to choose a target now work. The choice travels to the server and both players see the same thing happen, instead of only the person who made it.
- The turn clock only runs out on the player who actually has to choose.

## v0.77.79

## v0.77.78

## v0.77.70

- Fixed: the Cowardly Lion hurt itself the moment you played it. It now hurts itself when it conquers, which is what the card says.

## v0.77.61
- Five more abilities now come from the card sheet instead of the code: White Rabbit, Captain Hook, Aladdin, Guinevere and Sinbad.
- Sinbad's "Edgy" works for the first time: it was written on the card but had never been programmed.

## v0.77.60
- Groundwork for online play: the game and the server now work out what an ability does from the same single description, instead of each having its own copy of the rules.

## v0.77.59
- Abilities that only work from a certain turn now respect it: Strigoi gets its night bonus from turn 4, not from the first one.

## v0.77.58
- Card synergies now come from the card sheet too: "+1 for every Small on the board", "+2 to adjacent Wild allies" and the rest are read, not coded.
- Fixed: a card's ability was not travelling from the card list to the card you actually play, so several abilities quietly did nothing in a match.
- A random-side bonus now stays on the same side instead of jumping around, and in an online match both players see it on the same side.

## v0.77.57
- Card abilities are now written in the card sheet instead of in the game's code: what a card does is data, not a program.
- The rules that decide who can conquer whom — Excalibur, Shere Khan, Sleeping Beauty, Thumbelina, Merlin, The Crystal Princess — now come straight from the sheet.

## v0.77.56
- Your decks now live only on your account. They follow you to any computer, and clearing your browser data no longer touches them.
- Decks are no longer kept in the browser: a deck you build while signed out is not saved, and the game tells you so instead of losing it quietly.

## v0.77.55
- Online matches now end properly: the server decides who won and writes experience and rank for both players.
- A match only counts if both players' games agree on the board, turn by turn. If they stop agreeing, the match is stopped instead of quietly going wrong.
- Fixed: long online matches were being ended as "opponent left" while both players were still there.

## v0.77.54
- Matchmaking now starts a REAL online match: you and your opponent play on the same board, from two different computers.
- Your opponent's cards are not hidden, they are not there: their hand never leaves the server, and only becomes a real card the moment they play it.
- The board, who starts, the shuffle and the 60-second turn are all decided by the server, so both players always see the same match.
- Moves are checked by the server: playing out of turn, on a blocked or taken cell, or with a card you do not hold, is refused.
- If your opponent leaves, the match ends and says so.
## v0.77.53
- The player panels now show who is really playing: your name, your season rank and your avatar on the left, your opponent's on the right.
- The name plate no longer sits on a panel: the name reads directly on the banner.
