## v0.79.66
- The playtest agreement is at version 1.2, so everyone signs it once more. The text has not changed: the bump exists so that the prototype disclaimer added in the last version, which follows the signature, is seen by players who had already signed.
- The two columns of that disclaimer are twice as wide.
## v0.79.65
- A prototype disclaimer now follows the playtest agreement, once, on your first way in. Two columns: what we think about AI art, and what premium currency will and will not ever buy. Both say the same thing in the end, which is that what you are looking at is not finished and will never be for sale.
- Blocked tiles have new artwork. Five designs, and within a single match you will never see the same one twice.
## v0.79.64
- The ice on a card in your hand is a little larger, overlapping its edges. It read as a texture painted onto the card; now it reads as a block the card is caught inside.
## v0.79.63
- Frozen things are covered in ice. A frozen card in your hand and a frozen tile on the board now wear a real sheet of ice instead of a blue tint, laid over them at their exact size and blended so the art underneath still reads through. The ice follows whatever it covers: the card as it tilts in the fan and lifts under the cursor, the tile as it falls into place, shakes, or is carried off.
- Sherazade freezes a tile, at last. "Cliffhanger" has been on her card since the sheet described it, and nothing ever happened: the game declared it could only freeze a card in a hand, so her ability never even reached the engine. It now freezes an empty tile for one turn, and nothing can be played there while it lasts.
## v0.79.62
- The game no longer calls the GitHub API. It used to read the release notes and look for a newer build through an interface that allows 60 requests per hour per IP address, and it spent five of them on every page load plus thirty an hour while you played. Two open tabs, or a household behind one address, went over the limit; past it the notes and the version check went quiet with no explanation. The notes now come from this site, where the file has always been.
- The "A newer version is available" banner is gone. It had been searching the repository root for a numbered HTML file, and none has been published there since August 28th, so it never found anything; its Download button called a function that had already been removed. Nothing is lost that still worked. The check that stops you playing on an out-of-date client stays, and now costs nothing.
## v0.79.61
- The three letters answer the mouse. Hovering anywhere on one of the three now enlarges its envelope slightly, so it is clear which one you are about to pick.
- The currency icon flashes once when you spend, not twice. Two short pulses read as a tremor, as if the balance had been touched twice; one slightly longer flash says what actually happened.

## v0.79.60
- Your starter deck shows you its cards. When the letter opens, the "New deck unlocked!" panel no longer stands alone in the middle of the screen: it slides to the left and the twelve cards of the deck appear beside it, three per row, drawn in full like in the Library and scrollable with the wheel. Click one to hear it and see it up close. The frame and the title stay, and the title now says "New deck!".

## v0.79.59
- The turn clock never stops. Not while a card lands, not while it fights, and not while you are choosing a target for its ability: if the clock runs out before you choose, the turn ends and the ability goes unused. Until now placing a card froze the clock, and opening a target window started it again from the top.
- Online, a target window now actually times out. The line that closed it on expiry sat behind an early return for network matches — written correctly and never reached. A window nobody could close stayed open on both screens while the server moved on, and the next move landed on top of it. That was the crash behind Sea Witch, Mordred, and everyone else who asks you something.
- Sea Witch no longer breaks online matches on her own either: on your opponent's screen the cards in your hand are placeholders with no values, and her theft counted them differently on the two sides. Now she gains one point per card in your hand, the same number everywhere.
- What happens to a card while it sits in your hand now travels with it when you play it. Guinevere's gift, the Sea Witch's theft, the Genie's end-of-turn bonus: the other screen used to rebuild your card fresh from the catalogue and lose all of it, so the same card came down with two different numbers.
- The Magic Mirror copying the Genie no longer buffs the very same card and sides the Genie just buffed. Two abilities drawing "at random" in the same turn drew from the same seed; the drawing card is now part of it.
- White Rabbit starts in your hand in online matches too. The server deals the opening hand there, and it shuffled without looking.
- Cinderella becoming the Crystal Princess has the same scene as the Frog Prince: the jump, the flash. Her transformation ran through a door that changed the card without showing it.
- Rapunzel's pull no longer leaves a leftover on the pulled card that made its next animation start halfway through, sliding it back the way it came.
- Matchmaking will not start a fake match. When an opponent is found but the server cannot create the game, you are told so instead of being seated against an AI wearing their deck. The server now also checks both decks before creating the match.
