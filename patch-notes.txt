## v0.78.1
- Fixed: Strigoi transformed in your hand but not on the board. The flash played and the card stayed the same, because the transformation was still being asked for as if the card had just been played.
- A card that transforms now really becomes the other card: it takes its values, and keeps whatever buffs and debuffs it was carrying. Before, the new numbers lasted until the next redraw and then the old ones came back.

## v0.78.0
- Card transformations now come from the card sheet. Until today only two cards could transform, and not because of what their row said: their names were written into the game itself. Copying that syntax onto another card did nothing at all, and said nothing either.
- Strigoi becomes Dark Strigoi from turn 8, wherever it is — in your deck, in your hand or on the board.
- Cinderella now becomes The Crystal Princess whenever a Guardian ends up next to her, not only if one was already there when she was played. That is what her row says.
- A transformed card announces itself with its new voice, as it already did when kissed.

## v0.77.99
- Card artwork now appears immediately in the Library. Until today every player rediscovered, at every launch, which illustrations exist — about six hundred requests, made one by one, for an answer that is the same for everyone. The import now works it out once and writes it down with the cards.
- Takes effect after the next card import; until then the game keeps finding out on its own, as before.

## v0.77.98
- Fixed the attack that hit twice. It really was hitting twice: the striking card played its whole lunge, then played it again from the start, 0.19s later. At the moment of impact the game brings the defending card to the front, and doing that was picking up and re-attaching every card on the board — which restarts any animation in flight, including the attacker’s blow. Now only the one card moves.
- The same mistake was in three other places (a card landing, jumping, or trailing behind Rapunzel). All four now share one piece of code.

## v0.77.96
- Fixed: re-importing the cards from the sheet had stopped working. Two functions the importer borrows from the game had been removed in v0.77.91 as unused — they were not: their only caller lives in another program. No card data was affected, the import refuses to touch the database when it fails.

## v0.77.95
- A value’s colour now tells you the balance, not the last thing that happened to it: green if that group of values is above what the card started the match with, red if below, plain if it is back where it began. A card buffed twice and weakened once stays green.
- The last old-style buttons — in the Start Game window, the test-a-card window and the Book Packs page — now match every other button in the game, and about a hundred and sixty lines of styling that no longer applied to anything have been removed.
- Fixed: buttons marked as unavailable were drawn as if they were available.

## v0.77.94
- Testing a card from the debug menu now plays every card in that match at level 4, so you see the numbers and the abilities the card was actually written for. A level 1 card is not the card: it is its weakest version, and some abilities are still locked at that level.
- The debug menu has been redrawn in the same window and button style as the rest of the game.
- Two debug tools that had quietly lost their button — the asset previewer and the blur A/B test — can be reached again.

## v0.77.93
- Fixed: playing a second Genie gave its bonus to exactly the same value groups as the first one, and so did the third. The choice was made once per match and then repeated; now every play rolls again. Abilities that stay on a card, like the Pixies' bonus, still hold their group and do not jump around.
- Your name, level, experience, rank, avatar, currencies and decks now appear the moment you enter the main menu, instead of showing starting values for about ten seconds. Card artwork keeps loading quietly behind them.
- The main menu shows your real avatar instead of the placeholder one.

## v0.77.92
- Fixed: The Genie said it buffed every card in your hand, and it did — but the bonus landed on half a group of values, so on most cards it was invisible while still counting in a fight. A card's numbers come in groups, and any ability that touches them now always moves one whole group: a card showing 2, 4 and 6 buffed by 2 becomes 4-4-6, or 2-6-6, or 2-4-8. Never half of one.
- Fixed: several cards showed no preview of their values while you dragged them. Princess and the Pea, whose bonus depends on where you put her, was the clearest case; Snow White, Little Red Riding Hood, Lancelot, Strigoi and Pixies had the same problem. Dropping a card next to Baloo, Maid Marian, Little John, Dorothy Gale or Geppetto now previews the bonus they would give it, too.
- You can press Enter to log in or to create an account, instead of reaching for the button.
- Removed around seven hundred lines that used to read the cards from a Google spreadsheet. Nothing had called them since the cards moved to the database.
- Online: the server now knows how each card is divided into groups, so it works out the same numbers your game does.

## v0.77.90
- Nothing about you is kept in the browser any more. Your faction, volumes, settings, free-pack timer, Unicorn progress and which patch notes you have read all live on your account and follow you to any computer.
- Fixed, and it was a serious one: two windows open with two different accounts shared the same sign-in, so decks, levels, cards and currency from one account showed up in the other.
- Signing in is now per window and lasts as long as the window. You will be asked for your password each time you open the game — that is the price of never playing, or spending, on somebody else's account.
- The free pack's eight hours are now counted by the server, so clearing your browser data no longer earns you an extra pack, and two accounts on one computer no longer take the wait from each other.

## v0.77.89
- The game now loads what it will actually use: your real card list arrives while the loading bar is still running, so the artwork is ready when you reach the main menu instead of filling in afterwards.
- Signing in is immediate. Your avatar, level, experience, decks and card art are already there.
- The deck you had selected is the one you find selected next time, "random deck" included.
- Buff and malus are explained again when you hover a card or open it: one line per source, green when it raises a value and red when it lowers it. Abilities described in the card sheet are now included, which is most of them.
- Fixed: when you played as the second player, your deck pile and the "expand hand" hint stayed on your opponent's side of the screen.
- Attack: the landing bounce can no longer restart on top of the strike.

## v0.77.88

- Fixed: March Hare's swap played its animation and then quietly put the two values back where they were.
- Fixed: some cards showed no preview of their values while you dragged them over the board. Princess and the Pea, whose bonus depends on where you put her, showed nothing at all.
- Fixed: the expanded hand view was pushed off the left edge of the screen for one of the two players. Both players now see their expanded cards in the middle.
- Fixed: your deck's colours could show up on your opponent's side of an online match.
- When an ability finds nobody to target, the game now says which neighbours it looked at and why it passed on each of them.

## v0.77.54
