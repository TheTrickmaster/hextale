## v0.79.59
- The turn clock never stops, not even while you are choosing a target. If it runs out first, your turn ends and the ability goes unused.
- Online matches no longer get stuck when a card asks you to choose something. The window now times out on both screens.
- Sea Witch works online. She gains one point per card in your hand, the same number on both sides.
- What happens to a card in your hand now follows it onto the board online: Guinevere's gift, the Sea Witch's theft, the Genie's bonus.
- The Magic Mirror copying the Genie no longer hits the same card and the same sides the Genie just hit.
- White Rabbit starts in your hand in online matches too.
- Matchmaking no longer starts a fake match. If an opponent is found but the game cannot be created, you are told so.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements
