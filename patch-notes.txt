## v0.77.57
- Card abilities are now written in the card sheet instead of in the game's code: what a card does is data, not a program.
- The rules that decide who can conquer whom — Excalibur, Shere Khan, Sleeping Beauty, Thumbelina, Merlin, The Crystal Princess — now come straight from the sheet.

## v0.77.56
- Your decks now live only on your account. They follow you to any computer, and clearing your browser data no longer touches them.
- Decks are no longer kept in the browser: a deck you build while signed out is not saved, and the game tells you so instead of losing it quietly.

## v0.77.55
- Online matches now end properly: the server decides who won and writes experience and rank for both players.
- A match only counts if both players' games agree on the board, turn by turn. If they stop agreeing, the match is stopped instead of quietly going wrong.
- Fixed: long online matches were being ended as "opponent left" while both players were still there.

## v0.77.54
- Matchmaking now starts a REAL online match: you and your opponent play on the same board, from two different computers.
- Your opponent's cards are not hidden, they are not there: their hand never leaves the server, and only becomes a real card the moment they play it.
- The board, who starts, the shuffle and the 60-second turn are all decided by the server, so both players always see the same match.
- Moves are checked by the server: playing out of turn, on a blocked or taken cell, or with a card you do not hold, is refused.
- If your opponent leaves, the match ends and says so.
## v0.77.53
- The player panels now show who is really playing: your name, your season rank and your avatar on the left, your opponent's on the right.
- The name plate no longer sits on a panel: the name reads directly on the banner.

## v0.77.52
- The Matchmaking, Play vs AI and Campaign buttons look selected again only when they are selected: the unselected artwork had been overwritten with the selected one.
- Cards you keep when opening a Book Pack now really enter your collection, and stay there.
- Book Packs are drawn by the server, not by your game: what comes out of a pack is decided in one place, the same for everyone.
- Fairy dust and magic ink live on your account: they follow you to another computer and survive clearing your browser data. New players start with 100 of each.
- Keeping both cards from a pack is paid on the server, at the price shown on the button.
- Your collection no longer shows cards you do not own while the game is starting up: what you own was being read from the local copy only when the card list was missing.

## v0.77.51
- Matchmaking: "Find opponent" looks for a real player of a similar rank. The window widens by one rank every ten seconds, up to six, and among the players it can reach it picks the one whose deck is closest to yours in power and card level.
- While searching, the button reads "Cancel" and pressing it stops the search.
- If nobody turns up, a window says "No player found. Try again later."
- You play against your opponent's real deck, not a copy of yours.
- Decks that do not have exactly 12 cards, or that go over 24 power capacity, cannot be selected or played: not in Library & decks, not in matchmaking. They stay in the list, dimmed, so an incomplete deck never looks like a lost one.
- Messages and warnings now share a single window instead of one built for each occasion.

## v0.77.50
- The main menu now shows who you really are: your name, your level, your experience and your season rank, all kept on the server.
- Every match gives experience: 50 for a win, 20 for a loss. Coming back to the menu you see the bar glow and fill.
- Ranked matches move your season rank across twelve steps, from Bronze I to Gold Top. Reaching a new one is announced with its own window.
- Matches against the AI give experience but do not move your rank.

## v0.77.49
- Scelto "Random deck", la finestra di scelta del mazzo si riapre di nuovo.

## v0.77.48
- Anche l'arte delle carte viene precaricata: era l'ultima cosa che comparivano una alla volta, in partita.
- Il mazzo casuale ha il suo sfondo e sta in fondo alla lista, staccato dagli altri.
