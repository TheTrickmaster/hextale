## v0.79.60
- Your starter deck shows you its cards. When the letter opens, the "New deck unlocked!" panel no longer stands alone in the middle of the screen: it slides to the left and the twelve cards of the deck appear beside it, three per row, drawn in full like in the Library and scrollable with the wheel. Click one to hear it and see it up close. The frame and the title stay, and the title now says "New deck!".

## v0.79.59
- The turn clock never stops. Not while a card lands, not while it fights, and not while you are choosing a target for its ability: if the clock runs out before you choose, the turn ends and the ability goes unused. Until now placing a card froze the clock, and opening a target window started it again from the top.
- Online, a target window now actually times out. The line that closed it on expiry sat behind an early return for network matches — written correctly and never reached. A window nobody could close stayed open on both screens while the server moved on, and the next move landed on top of it. That was the crash behind Sea Witch, Mordred, and everyone else who asks you something.
- Sea Witch no longer breaks online matches on her own either: on your opponent's screen the cards in your hand are placeholders with no values, and her theft counted them differently on the two sides. Now she gains one point per card in your hand, the same number everywhere.
- What happens to a card while it sits in your hand now travels with it when you play it. Guinevere's gift, the Sea Witch's theft, the Genie's end-of-turn bonus: the other screen used to rebuild your card fresh from the catalogue and lose all of it, so the same card came down with two different numbers.
- The Magic Mirror copying the Genie no longer buffs the very same card and sides the Genie just buffed. Two abilities drawing "at random" in the same turn drew from the same seed; the drawing card is now part of it.
- White Rabbit starts in your hand in online matches too. The server deals the opening hand there, and it shuffled without looking.
- Cinderella becoming the Crystal Princess has the same scene as the Frog Prince: the jump, the flash. Her transformation ran through a door that changed the card without showing it.
- Rapunzel's pull no longer leaves a leftover on the pulled card that made its next animation start halfway through, sliding it back the way it came.
- Matchmaking will not start a fake match. When an opponent is found but the server cannot create the game, you are told so instead of being seated against an AI wearing their deck. The server now also checks both decks before creating the match.

## v0.79.58
- In an online match, the loser used to hear the victory fanfare and the winner the defeat one — and the two scores on the end screen were swapped for one of the two players. The server tells each client which of the two numbers it is, and that number was written onto the game state a moment before the state was rebuilt from scratch for the new match. It was thrown away every time, so both clients believed they were player one.
- Playing a card that asks you to pick a target no longer hands you a fresh turn. The countdown is stopped while the card resolves and was being STARTED again to keep the choice window alive, which reset the clock and refilled the bar. It now resumes from where it stopped. Online it was already right, because there the clock belongs to the server.

## v0.79.57
- Turns last 45 seconds instead of a minute.

## v0.79.56
- Cards on the board are worth points at the end of every turn according to their rarity, and the scale runs backwards: a common is worth 3, a rare 2, a mythic 1, a timeless nothing. Two commons, a mythic and a timeless on the board is seven points.
- So a hand of common cards is a choice now, not a consolation. Fill the board with legendaries and you win the exchanges and score nothing; fill it with commons and you score and lose the exchanges. The board is the question.
- A timeless card does not flash in the end-of-turn wave at all. Lighting it up while the counter stayed put would read as a fault rather than as the rule.

## v0.79.53
- The Library fills in one card at a time now: a quick fade each, fifty milliseconds apart. A hundred and eleven cards arriving in the same frame is a wall; arriving in order, they read as a list. Sorting, filtering and flipping between dark and light stay instant — those are moments when you are comparing something, and a wave would just be a wait.

## v0.79.52
- The catalogue has been rebuilt and now knows all 109 illustrations and all 43 battlecries, so the game reads them instead of hunting for them: the art is there the moment the Library opens, for every card, and the cards shout again.
- One more guard of my own had to go. Yesterday I made the game distrust a catalogue that carried empty voice lists, because an empty list was the fingerprint of the failed import. But no card in this game has numbered voice lines — only battlecries — so an empty list is the true answer, and a perfect catalogue would have been distrusted too. The question is now the right one: did the import look for audio at all?

## v0.79.51
- Found why four cards showed their art instantly and the other hundred took several seconds. The game looks for a file next to itself first and on the website second, and the "next to itself" half had been pointing at a folder that does not exist ever since the game moved into its own directory. Everything went to the network, including the tool that builds the card catalogue: it asked the website about 1300 questions in one breath, the website started answering "too many requests", and the tool could not tell that answer apart from "there is no such file". So it wrote down that 107 cards have no illustration and that no card has a battlecry, and every player has been rediscovering the truth the slow way at every launch, one throttled request at a time.
- The four instant ones were simply the four that squeezed through before the throttling began.
- The game now warns in its console if the local folder never answers, instead of quietly downloading everything it already has on disk.
