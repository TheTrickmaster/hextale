## v0.79.90
- Cards now have levels. Every copy of a card you keep from a pack counts toward its next level: 2 copies for level 2, 5 for level 3 and 9 for level 4.

- Leveling up costs 50, 150 and 400 Magic Ink for Common cards, 100, 300 and 800 for Rare, 150, 450 and 1200 for Mythic, and 200, 600 and 1600 for Timeless.
- Starter deck cards count as one copy each. If you already had duplicates, your cards have been raised to the level they earned, for free.
- Once a card has all 9 copies, any further copy you keep turns into Magic Ink.
- When you open a pack, each card shows its level and how many copies it needs for the next one.
- Card levels apply in online matches too: each of your opponent's cards plays at its own level.
- Minor bug fixes and improvements
