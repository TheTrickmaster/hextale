Card power

A card can have 2 to 4 different values on it. The sides of the hexagon affected by a value are divided by lines that cross its artwork.

For example, this card here has a value of 12 on the two sides on the right, 8 on the two on the left and 6 on the two at the bottom.

Level

You can level up a card up to level 4.

Each level grants the card a buff on its power and in some cases can unlock game-changing abilities!

A card that looks weak on level 1 can become extremely strong or useful if you level it up enough.

Ability description / Flavor text

A card with ability has the ability name and description written at its bottom. A card without ability, on the other hand, will show a flavor text with a quote of the character.

Traits

Traits are mostly determined by a character’s lore and story. Learn to use them in combo with powerful abilities to destroy your opponent!

Tap the card

Tap any part of the card to find out what it is and what it does.

Build the definitive deck

Carefully select the best cards that suit your playstyle the most. Aggro, defensive, RnD, glass cannon, assassin, magic, trap, and many more.

Compose a deck of 12 cards to play against your opponents and show no mercy.

You need to be smart and predict the moves of your opponent: if your cards get conquered, they will no longer be yours (until the end of the game, of course!).

With our power capacity system, there are virtually infinite deck combinations!

Fight against other players

The battlefield is made of 19 hexagonal tiles that serve infinite combinations.

Every match generates a unique one.

The rules are simple: play a card with a higher power than your opponent to conquer it and make it yours, play defensively or setup game-breaking traps.

Every match is unique, every strategy is allowed.
